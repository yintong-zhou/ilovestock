# Brand Guidelines — iLoveStock

> Generated with Brand Spec Sheet on 10/20/2026.

## The project

- **Name**: iLoveStock
- **Description**: Educational screening tool for investment in stock assets. tecnical and fundamentals analysis
- **Target audience**: people who love stock investment, age from 18 to 50
- **Sector**: Finance
- **Mood**: Trustworthy

## Colors

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Primary | `#0B3D62` |
| Secondary | `#1F6F8B` |
| Accent | `#2E9E5B` |
| Dark neutral | `#16232E` |
| Light neutral | `#EEF3F6` |

## Typography

- **Headings**: Merriweather, weight 700
- **Body**: Source Sans 3, weight 400
- Keep body copy around 60–70 characters per line for readability.

## Tone of voice

`transparent` · `steady` · `consistent` · `reassuring` · `competent`

## Imagery & iconography

Real people in professional settings, data and charts presented clearly, consistent line iconography. Avoid ambiguous imagery or anything that manufactures urgency. Use real (anonymized) data patterns in charts rather than generic upward-trending mockups.

## Spacing & grid

- **Base unit**: 8px
- **Scale**: 8px, 16px, 24px, 40px, 64px
- **Corner radius**: 4–6px
- Rigid, symmetrical grid, consistent alignment, a highly legible visual hierarchy.

## Logo — clear space

The logo always appears in the same position (top left) across digital materials. Clear space equal to the cap-height of the mark.

Files (256 px, cropped to the mark):

- `web/public/logo.png`: transparent background. Web app (sidebar, PIN screen, favicon), README.
- `desktop/logo.png`: the same file, for the desktop app (window and installer icon, first-PIN screen).
- `src/ilovestock/logo.png`: white background. PDF documents (History and Watchlist), top left of every page.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Keep every material consistent
- Present data with clean charts
- Show real outcomes, not projections
- Show fees, risks, and terms with the same visual weight as benefits

**Avoid:**
- Make promises the product doesn't keep
- Hide important terms in fine print
- Use unsubstantiated superlatives
- Use countdowns, alarms, or language that manufactures urgency
